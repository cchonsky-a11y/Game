# P0_PROPOSALS.md — Values and rules proposed during the P0 build

> SYSTEMS.md and PROTOTYPE_SCOPE.md leave many P0 numbers and small rules open (marked "tune" or not specified).
> CLAUDE.md rule 3 says to stop and ask when a rule is missing. To keep the build moving, each gap was filled with
> the smallest reasonable **placeholder**, recorded here, and referenced from `data/tuning.json` as `PROPOSED P0-xx`.
>
> **Nothing here is canonical until Corey approves it.** On approval, move the rule into SYSTEMS.md, the GDD
> (and Appendix A where relevant) and DECISIONS.md per BUILD_GUIDE.md §2.1. On rejection, change tuning.json or
> content and the affected tests. Values tuned after the first batch run are marked **(batch)**; values set by the 2026-09-26 review are marked **(decided)**.

## Decisions from the 2026-09-26 review

| # | Question | Decision | Where it lives now |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Debt compounding across the absence | Compounding stops 30 years after departure. A crisis still clears all debt and resets expectations. The rarer-recurrence damping was removed. The jump confirmation still lists debts and their projected growth. | tuning `debt.compoundingCapYearsAfterDeparture`; `jump.crisisChancePerYear` restored to 1% / 3% / 6% / 10% |
| 2 | Governance and Economy crises | Plague odds (warnings and recurrences) use **Medicine** debt only. Governance and Economy debt tiers raise plague **severity** instead. | tuning `plague.severityPerTier` (amounts are placeholders, P0-09) |
| 3 | Legend stand-in | Accepted, **P0-only**: a loyal leader (≥ 70) at departure counts as "keeping the Legend alive". | tuning `institutions.strongMinLoyalty` |
| 4 | Balance gate | 65% cap per timing (early and late separately), plus a timing gate: neither timing wins more than 65% of runs across all strategies. | PROTOTYPE_SCOPE pass criteria; batch report |
| 5 | Legacy formula | Not raised in the review; still unbuilt (out of P0 scope). | — |
| 6 | Pacing | **P0-only:** 6-month turns, 20 per era, auto-advance of turns with no pending decision, Attention demand ≥ 1.5× supply (122 vs 80). | PROTOTYPE_SCOPE; tuning `time.*`, Attention costs |
| — | Institution gold and corruption | New P0 features (review sections B and C), approved into SYSTEMS.md §7 and §9 on 2026-09-26. | SYSTEMS.md; PROTOTYPE_SCOPE; P0-15 |
