# P0_PROPOSALS.md — Values and rules proposed during the P0 build

> SYSTEMS.md and PROTOTYPE_SCOPE.md leave many P0 numbers and small rules open (marked "tune" or not specified).
> CLAUDE.md rule 3 says to stop and ask when a rule is missing. To keep the build moving, each gap was filled with
> the smallest reasonable **placeholder**, recorded here, and referenced from `data/tuning.json` as `PROPOSED P0-xx`.
>
> **Nothing here is canonical until Corey approves it.** On approval, move the rule into SYSTEMS.md, the GDD and
> DECISIONS.md per BUILD_GUIDE.md §2.1. On rejection, change tuning.json/content and the affected tests.

| ID | Area | Proposal | Why a placeholder was needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| P0-01 | Time | Scenario starts in month 1 (Ianuarius) of AD 155. | No start month specified. |
| P0-02 | Domains | Levels run 0–100. Start: Medicine 50, Governance 55, Economy 55 (equal to the AD 155 benchmark, so the starting Index is 100). | No start levels or scale specified. |
| P0-03 | History | Authored Rome curves per domain (see `history.*` in tuning.json), interpolated linearly: dips for the Antonine Plague (165–180), the Plague of Cyprian (~250) and the Crisis of the Third Century (235–284), recovery under Diocletian (~300), decline to AD 405. Used both as the §6 benchmark and the §12 Index denominator. | SYSTEMS §6 and §12 need historical values; GDD §14 says early values are authored estimates. |
| P0-04 | Priorities | Yearly level change by priority: Protect +1, Maintain −2, Accept Risk −8. | SYSTEMS §5 says levels decay without upkeep but gives no rates. Accept Risk −8 was chosen so accepted risk reaches Strained in 2 years (pass criterion: 2–3). |
| P0-05 | Expectations | The recent peak fades 5 points per year, never below the current level. | SYSTEMS §6 "recent peak fading over time" has no rate. |
| P0-06 | Debt tiers | Strained ≥ 20, Fragile ≥ 60, Critical ≥ 120 debt. | SYSTEMS §6 names tiers without thresholds. The GDD example (≈63 after 3 years) lands in Fragile, as the GDD states. |
| P0-07 | Gold | Start 60 gold. Yearly income = 20 + 0.6 × Economy level + project bonuses, settled each turn pro rata. Upkeep per domain per year: Protect 20, Maintain 8, Accept Risk 0; institutions are paid first and unpaid domain upkeep acts as Accept Risk for its share. Prevention costs 2 gold per level point, so paydown costs 3 gold per debt point (the §6 1.5×). | SYSTEMS §5/§9 give the structure (upkeep split by priority, simple income) but no amounts. |
| P0-08 | Projects | 9 one-time projects, 3 per domain, in `data/content/projects.json` (cost 40–70 gold, 1 Attention per turn of work, 1–3 turns, +5 to +12 level, some with extras: income, plague resilience, institution loyalty/strength, clean water). | SCOPE lists examples only. |
| P0-09 | Plague | Warning 1 appears in AD 161 or 162 (seeded). Each later year a warning advances with a chance set by the region's **worst** debt tier (Stable 35%, Strained 55%, Fragile 75%, Critical 95%), +10% while the fountain is foul, and certainly from AD 165. At most one stage per year, so all three warnings are always seen before the outbreak (AD 164–168). Severity = Hazard × Exposure × (1 − Resilience) (the §9 loss form): Hazard = 50 + 0.3 × Medicine debt (+10 with foul water); Resilience = 0.5 × Medicine/100 + 0.25 × Governance/100 + preparations + response + institution. Damage to levels, gold and population scales with severity; the crisis releases 60% of each domain's debt; one opening is drawn by weighted seeded choice (orphaned talent, labor scarcity, new movement, experimentation). Responses: quarantine, hospice (needs the physicians' circle), or none. | SYSTEMS §6 gives the structure (tiers raise chance, severity scales with debt, warnings, openings) without numbers. **Open question:** in P0 the plague is the only crisis, so it uses the worst tier across all three domains. Governance and Economy debt otherwise have no in-scope crisis. |
| P0-10 | Institutions | Found 50 gold + 1 Attention (strength 50, loyalty 60); charter 30 gold + 1 Attention; endow 60 gold (ends the 8-gold yearly upkeep). Oversee: 1 Attention, +8 loyalty, +2 strength, once per turn each. In-era loyalty fades 4/yr; strength +4/yr at loyalty ≥ 50, else −4/yr. The circle adds up to +0.15 plague resilience (× strength) and enables the hospice response. **Quality:** "chartered and endowed" needs both; "strong" also needs a thriving region (Index ≥ 100) and — standing in for the out-of-scope Legend — leader loyalty ≥ 70 at departure. During absence, strength and loyalty both decay at the §7 rate. | SYSTEMS §7 gives decay rates and attributes, not costs or in-era dynamics. **Open question:** SYSTEMS §7's "strong" tier requires "keeping the Legend alive", but Legend is out of P0 scope. |
| P0-11 | Drift | Drift per decade = 4 (× 0.5 if chartered, × 0.5 again if strong) + seeded 0–3, +4 if loyalty < 40; the institution takes its drift path at 60. The path is fixed at departure by pre-authored conditions: Circle → Hospice of the Fountain (charitable) if the promise was kept or the Circle ran the plague hospice, otherwise the Collegium of Physicians (commercial). Faction → Reform Bench (reformist) if chartered and Governance is at or above the benchmark, otherwise the Caecilian Houses (politically powerful, arrives as **Captured**). Dissolved below strength 10; Rogue below loyalty 15. Leaders are succeeded every 3 decades. | SCOPE asks for 2 pre-authored drift paths each; no selection or timing rule is given. |
| P0-12 | Attention, seeded choice, promise | **Attention:** 4 per turn; multi-turn projects and commitments reserve Attention in later turns. The one personal action is *work* (1 Attention, +10 gold). **Multi-turn commitment (switchable in tuning):** mentor an institution, 1 Attention for 3 turns, then +12 strength and +6 loyalty. **Seeded choice:** offered in turn 1 using the normal project prices (starting gold covers only one); it lapses to "neither" after turn 1. It pays off in AD 157: a fever (Medicine −5) if the fountain is still broken, and the smith leaving (Economy −4) if the workshop is still unfunded. Both are logged with the choice as their cause. **Promise:** with the first plague warning, Demetria asks the inventor to stay until the sickness has passed. Accepting gives +8 Circle loyalty and refusing gives −5; keeping it gives +12; jumping before the plague passes breaks it (−30). | SCOPE lists these features without numbers or timing. |
| P0-13 | Jump and arrival | Absence = 250 years in 25 decade steps. Each decade, a domain moves 15% of the way toward its historical baseline **plus** 0.3 × the strength of any surviving institution that maintains it (Circle → Medicine, faction → Governance); then 10 yearly steps of the §6 debt rule run. If the Antonine plague hasn't happened yet, it keeps its yearly rules, and the institutions respond on their own. After it, the same crisis can recur with a yearly chance by worst tier (1% / 3% / 6% / 10%). Unfinished projects are abandoned. The fountain loses 15 condition per decade unless the Circle (strength ≥ 20) or healthy Medicine keeps it up. **Echoes:** (1) the seeded choice, (2) the Circle, else the faction, else "Demetria and Varro, never backed", (3) the promise. **Beats:** Recognition = seeded choice; Wrongness = the domain furthest from history (or "as history" within ±8); Personal echo = the promise; Discovery = institution outcomes. Learn more = Index before and after, institution outcomes, and crises (facts only). | SCOPE fixes the structure; coarse-mode rates and beat selection are not specified. **Open question:** 5% compounding over 250 years makes any debt left at departure huge unless a crisis releases it. Is that intended (so paying down debt before leaving becomes a key decision)? |
