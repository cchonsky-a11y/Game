# P0_PROPOSALS.md — Values and rules proposed during the P0 build

> SYSTEMS.md and PROTOTYPE_SCOPE.md leave many P0 numbers and small rules open (marked "tune" or not specified).
> CLAUDE.md rule 3 says to stop and ask when a rule is missing. To keep the build moving, each gap was filled with
> the smallest reasonable **placeholder**, recorded here, and referenced from `data/tuning.json` as `PROPOSED P0-xx`.
>
> **Nothing here is canonical until Corey approves it.** On approval, move the rule into SYSTEMS.md, the GDD
> (and Appendix A where relevant) and DECISIONS.md per BUILD_GUIDE.md §2.1. On rejection, change tuning.json or
> content and the affected tests. Values tuned after the first batch run are marked **(batch)**; values set by the 2026-09-26 review are marked **(decided)**.

## Decisions from the 2026-09-26 review

| # | Question | Decision | Where it lives now |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Debt compounding across the absence | Compounding stops 30 years after departure. A crisis still clears all debt and resets expectations. The rarer-recurrence damping was removed. The jump confirmation still lists debts and their projected growth. | tuning `debt.compoundingCapYearsAfterDeparture`; `jump.crisisChancePerYear` restored to 1% / 3% / 6% / 10% |
| 2 | Governance and Economy crises | Plague odds (warnings and recurrences) use **Medicine** debt only. Governance and Economy debt tiers raise plague **severity** instead. | tuning `plague.severityPerTier` (amounts are placeholders, P0-09) |
| 3 | Legend stand-in | Accepted, **P0-only**: a loyal leader (≥ 70) at departure counts as "keeping the Legend alive". | tuning `institutions.strongMinLoyalty` |
| 4 | Balance gate | 65% cap per timing (early and late separately), plus a timing gate: neither timing wins more than 65% of runs across all strategies. | PROTOTYPE_SCOPE pass criteria; batch report |
| 5 | Legacy formula | Not raised in the review; still unbuilt (out of P0 scope). | — |
| 6 | Pacing | **P0-only:** 6-month turns, 20 per era, auto-advance of turns with no pending decision, Attention demand ≥ 1.5× supply (122 vs 80). | PROTOTYPE_SCOPE; tuning `time.*`, Attention costs |
| — | Institution gold and corruption | New P0 features (review sections B and C). | PROTOTYPE_SCOPE; P0-15 |

| P0-09 | Plague | Warning 1 appears in AD 158 or 159 (seeded; moved earlier so the outbreak falls inside the 20-turn era). Each later year a warning advances with a chance set by **Medicine's** debt tier **(decided)** (Stable 35%, Strained 55%, Fragile 75%, Critical 95%), +10% while the fountain is foul, and certainly from AD 162. At most one stage per year, so all three warnings are always seen before the outbreak (AD 161–164). Governance and Economy tiers multiply severity by 1 + (0 / 0.1 / 0.2 / 0.35 each, Stable to Critical) **(decided; amounts proposed)**. Severity = Hazard × Exposure × (1 − Resilience) (the §9 loss form): Hazard = 50 + 0.3 × Medicine debt (+10 with foul water, max 100); Resilience = 0.5 × Medicine/100 + 0.25 × Governance/100 + preparations + response + 0.15 × Circle strength/100 (max 0.85). Level damage = severity × 0.25 / 0.1 / 0.25 (Medicine / Governance / Economy) **(batch)**; gold loss = severity; deaths = 0.3% of population per severity point. The crisis releases **all** debt and resets expectations **(decided)**. One opening is drawn by weighted seeded choice (orphaned talent, labor scarcity, new movement, experimentation). Responses: quarantine (20 gold, 2 Attention, +0.12), hospice (needs the Circle; 30 gold, 2 Attention, +0.15), or none. | SYSTEMS §6 gives the structure (tiers raise chance, severity scales with debt, warnings, openings) without numbers. |
| P0-10 | Institutions | Found 50 gold + 2 Attention (strength 50, loyalty 60); charter 30 gold + 2 Attention; endow any amount for 1 Attention (holdings ≥ 60 make it endowed and end the 8-gold yearly upkeep). Oversee: 1 Attention, +8 loyalty, +2 strength, once per turn each. In-era loyalty fades 10/yr (Attention demand); strength +4/yr at loyalty ≥ 50, else −4/yr. The circle adds plague resilience and enables the hospice response. **Quality:** "chartered and endowed" needs both; "strong" also needs a thriving region (Index ≥ 100) and leader loyalty ≥ 70 at departure (**decided, P0-only** stand-in for the Legend). During absence, strength and loyalty both decay at the §7 rate. | SYSTEMS §7 gives decay rates and attributes, not costs or in-era dynamics. |
| P0-11 | Drift | Drift per decade = 4 (× 0.5 if chartered, × 0.5 again if strong) + seeded 0–3, +4 if loyalty < 40; the institution takes its drift path at 60. The path is fixed at departure by pre-authored conditions: Circle → Hospice of the Fountain (charitable) if the promise was kept or the Circle ran the plague hospice, otherwise the Collegium of Physicians (commercial). Faction → Reform Bench (reformist) if chartered and Governance is at or above the benchmark, otherwise the Caecilian Houses (politically powerful, arrives as **Captured**). Dissolved below strength 10; Rogue below loyalty 15. Leaders are succeeded every 3 decades. | SCOPE asks for 2 pre-authored drift paths each; no selection or timing rule is given. |
| P0-12 | Attention, seeded choice, promise | **Attention:** 4 per turn; multi-turn projects and commitments reserve Attention in later turns. Every project costs 2 Attention per turn of work over 1–4 turns **(tuned for decided demand ≥ 1.5× supply)**. The one personal action is *work* (1 Attention, +5 gold). **Multi-turn commitment (switch in tuning):** mentor an institution, 2 Attention for 5 turns, then +12 strength and +6 loyalty. **Seeded choice:** open in turns 1–2 at normal project prices (starting gold covers only one), then it lapses to "neither". It pays off in AD 157: a fever (Medicine −5) if the fountain is still broken, and the smith leaving (Economy −4) if the workshop is still unfunded. Both are logged with the choice as their cause. **Promise:** with the first plague warning, Demetria asks the inventor to stay until the sickness has passed. Accepting gives +8 Circle loyalty and refusing gives −5; keeping it gives +12; jumping before the plague passes breaks it (−30). | SCOPE lists these features without numbers or timing. |
| P0-13 | Jump and arrival | Absence = 250 years in 25 decade steps. Each decade, a domain moves 50% of the way **(batch)** toward its historical baseline **plus** 0.3 × the strength of any surviving institution that maintains it (Circle → Medicine, faction → Governance); then 10 yearly steps of the §6 debt rule run. If the Antonine plague hasn't happened yet, it keeps its yearly rules and institutions respond on their own (hospice if the Circle is loyal ≥ 50, else quarantine if the faction is). After it, the same crisis can recur with a yearly chance by **Medicine's** tier: 1% / 3% / 6% / 10% **(decided: batch damping removed)**. Debt compounds only in the first 30 years **(decided)**. Population recovers 20% per decade toward its old size (flavor). Unfinished projects are abandoned. The fountain loses 15 condition per decade unless the Circle (strength ≥ 20) or healthy Medicine keeps it up. **Echoes:** (1) the seeded choice, (2) the Circle, else the faction, else "Demetria and Varro, never backed", (3) the promise. **Beats:** Recognition = seeded choice; Wrongness = the domain furthest from history (or "as history" within ±8); Personal echo = the promise; Discovery = institution outcomes. Learn more = Index before and after, institution outcomes, crises (facts only). The jump confirmation lists what is left behind (exposures, never outcomes). | SCOPE fixes the structure; coarse-mode rates and beat selection are not specified. |
| P0-14 | Batch runner | **Balanced:** fountain; Maintain everywhere; finish the Circle (found, charter, endow) before the faction; projects for the weakest domain; oversee below loyalty 65; pay down Strained debt; before jumping, pay down all debt, audit both institutions and split the remaining gold between them as endowments; accept the promise; hospice, else quarantine. **Specialized:** fountain; Protect Medicine; Circle only, with mentoring; Medicine projects only; before jumping, pay down all debt, audit the Circle and endow it with the rest. **Neglectful:** workshop; Accept Risk everywhere; no projects or institutions; refuse the promise; no response. **Timing:** Early = leave at the start of AD 160 (before the outbreak); Late = at the start of AD 165 (the era's 20 turns are over). **Win** = highest arrival Index among the three strategies on the same seed and timing (ties split). **Timing gate (decided):** for each strategy and seed, the timing with the higher arrival Index wins. | SCOPE names the strategies and timings, not their scripts or the win measure. |
