THE BUTTERFLY EFFECT — P0 Butterfly Test
========================================
Rome, AD 155. Your time machine failed and left you here. You have a little gold, what you know,
and a small circle of people willing to listen. Something is coming from the East in a few years.
When you're ready, the machine can carry you 250 years forward. What you leave behind will go on without you.

First, a choice. You can afford only one:
  choose fountain  — The Subura's fountain runs brown. Clear the lead pipe and reline the basin.
  choose workshop  — Equip the smith by the Porta Trigemina with a second forge and paid apprentices.
Type 'help' for commands.

What you leave behind (5.5 gold in hand):
  • No institution will look after Rome while you're away.

Prepare: paydown <domain> <points> · endow <inst> <gold|all> · audit <inst>. Type 'jump' again to go, or anything else to stay.

> jump

The machine shudders. Decades pass in the dark...

— Recognition —
You climb out of the machine in AD 415. The Tiber smells the same. You find the district fountain you
repaired by the shape of the street, but the basin is cracked and dry, full of leaves. Of the smith
you didn't choose, there is no trace.

— Wrongness —
Something is wrong. Whole blocks of the Subura have fallen in, the streets are half-empty, and people
cover their mouths when strangers pass.

— Personal echo —
In an old house in the Subura you find a line carved over a door, in Demetria's Greek: 'For the one
who stayed.' They don't know it means you.

— Discovery —
You ask after Demetria of Pergamon and Senator Varro. No one has heard either name. Nothing you built
outlasted you.
