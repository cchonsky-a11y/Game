THE BUTTERFLY EFFECT — P0 Butterfly Test
========================================
Rome, AD 155. Your time machine malfunctioned and stranded you here. You have a pouch of gold you scavenged from the
machine (every bit of it must go back before it can fly), what you know, and no one who owes you anything.
You don't yet know what broke. Something is coming from the East within ten years.
Rome runs on silver denarii, not gold: change your aurei at the money changers (exchange <n> aurei). Gold holds its value; the denarius doesn't.
Once you repair it, the machine can carry you forward, 25 to 60 years depending on how well you repair it. What you leave behind will go on without you.

This prototype covers one era, a jump and your arrival, and then, if you like, one more jump to see how it all aged. The test ends there.

First, a choice. You can afford only one:
  choose fountain  — The Subura's fountain runs brown. Clear the lead pipe and reline the basin.
  choose workshop  — Pay for a second forge and paid apprentices at the smith's by the Porta Trigemina, in exchange for a share of the business.
Your ears still ring from the crash. Before anything else you could walk and get your bearings: visit market, changers, forges, curia or subura (no Attention).
Type 'help' for commands.
