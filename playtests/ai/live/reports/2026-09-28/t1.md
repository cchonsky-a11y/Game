# Playtest report: t1

**Persona:** An experienced Civilization / Paradox player who likes to optimize and plan ahead.
**Seed:** 611 (final hash d6a7eb1b3ecf19bc)
**Length:** about 150 commands. Rome era AD 155 to 174 (all 120 turns), then two jumps (AD 174 → 234 → 274).

## Key choices
- **Hour one (AD 155):** I chose the workshop over the fountain. I read it as the economic engine: it unlocks the guild, the Junians and the inventions. I then repaired the fountain myself as a project in AD 156, which let me join the Physicians' Circle.
- **Institutions:** I joined the Guild (155), the Circle (156) and the Junians (157). I reached 10% of each mostly through seniority and attending meetings. The Circle went to 25% in 165 (through the spirits invention) and 40% by 170. I became senior physician in 162 and second to the archiater in 166. I voted Hospice (Circle), Junian Bench and Free Traders every time. I declined the guild's quaestor office every year. I founded the School of the Fountain in 172 and invested until its strength was 25.4.
- **Inventions:** treadle lathe (157), soap (158), distilled wine (164).
- **Workshop:** hired a second apprentice (160) and later lost one when I couldn't pay him in 165. Expanded to a yard (160). Tertius: sold the designs to the guild (163). The river forge was funded by a guild loan (168).
- **Rome's choices:** Tiber flood 161, relief. Parthian levy 162, ducked. Galen 162, sponsored him. Sabinus's grain cartel 164, refused and spoke for open prices. Palace auction 169, none (couldn't afford it). Invasion scare 170, held firm and bought. Late grain fleet 172, it lapsed while I wasn't paying attention.
- **Promise:** yes, stay through the plague (165). Kept.
- **Plague:** quarantine rules in place by 164. Response was the hospice. 2% died against history's 10%. The Plague of Cyprian (251) also killed 2%, against history's 8%.
- **Policy:** none. I never had a Governance voice and didn't advocate.
- **Priorities:** Medicine Protect (165) and Economy Protect (171).
- **Jump:** at the calendar's end, AD 174, with all 3 upgrades (range 50–60). I carried 8 aurei. Nothing was endowed and nothing was chartered.

## The four questions
1. **What were you choosing between?** Money now versus standing and influence later. Crafting and ironwork versus attending meetings, versus inventions that took Attention for several turns. Medicine versus trade: quarantine hurt Ostia, and so did the Galen-versus-Circle choice. Staying loyal to the Circle's hospice camp versus spreading myself across the guild and the Junians. Leaving early with a short jump versus staying for upgrades and more legacy. Near the end: charter, endow, or found a school with the little money I had.
2. **What changed in the world you came back to?** In AD 274 Rome is bigger (1.43M), healthier and cleaner. The fountain still runs, and healers use soap and distilled wine. My forge became a whole street down to the river, with lathes everywhere and the Tertian ship-fitting pattern across the Mediterranean. The Circle survived as the Hospice of the Fountain and still has my name on its roll. The guild became a grain cartel, the Junians became rogue landlords, and my school vanished. The coin is 3% silver. The Index was 153.
3. **Did any of it feel connected to you?** Yes, strongly. The stayer's supper and my worn name on the roll connected to the promise I kept. The Hospice leading the Circle connected to my 40 votes and my last orders. The street of forges came from the hour-one workshop choice. The guild turning into the cartel came from the loan I let Tertius take from them, and from Sabinus, who never forgave me for refusing him.
4. **Would you jump again?** Yes, and badly. That's partly because a bug stole my first arrival, but also because I want to see whether the Hospice lasts and whether the cartel breaks.

## Bugs, dead ends and confusing text
- **Major: a double `jump` skips the first arrival.** `jump` shows the briefing. A second `jump` departs ("It carries you 60 years"). A third `jump`, typed right after, immediately fired the second jump ("It carries you 40 years") with no arrival shown in between. After the first departure the output showed no arrival text, only the jump line. The arrival then refers to "40 years after your last visit" and "when you left" (AD 234), a visit I never saw. `learn more` shows "Departure, AD 234". There should be a confirmation, and the first arrival should always print.
- **Hospice response cost gold without warning.** "Response chosen: hospice. It takes effect at the end of the turn." gave no price, but it took roughly 30 aurei (about 880 denarii). That left me at 0, so all three institutions reported "You couldn't pay what you owe... no seniority this year". `why plague` listed deaths per response but no costs.
- **"Profit twice" triggered without profiteering:** "The Subura has seen you profit from its bad years twice. Children throw mud at your door; buyers find other sellers." The cause shown was the Tertius river-forge guild loan. My other answers were relief, duck, sponsor, refuse, sell designs.
- **The briefing suggests actions you can't take:** "(endow circle <denarii>)" appears while I held 40%. Then: "You hold 40% of the Circle; you need 50% to direct it".
- **Paydown auto-ends the turn:** after `paydown economy 2`, "(No Attention left: the turn ends.)" So my next `end` skipped a turn with 3 Attention unused. The turn-end behaviour is inconsistent: sometimes it ends when Attention hits 0, sometimes it waits for 'end' ("You can still pay down debt").
- **Arrival inconsistencies:** "The fountain you repaired late still runs" (I repaired it in AD 156, very early). "keep a copy of your charter under glass" (I never chartered the Circle). At the forges: "The Ostia Grain Cartel: drifted, strength 14." appears oddly inside the forges description.
- **Text issues:** "the guild:  (if you're a member)" is empty in the inventions list. "the Hospice of the Fountain lead, as you want." has a lowercase start. "Your work as senior physician of the Circle keep Medicine up" should be "keeps". Parts of the log's gold changes show in aurei units ("[-30]") while the rest of the UI uses denarii.
- **Odd causality:** the Circle's seniority gain was shown "because: The Tiber floods: Demetria... gives you a larger say".
- **Camp leadership is opaque:** the guild shows "cartel lead; your votes 9–0" with nothing explaining why my votes don't count.
- **School founding is a near-trap:** strength 25.4 at departure against 10% bare decay a decade meant it Dissolved. The founding text warns about "below strength 25", but not that decay puts it below that line straight away.
- **Pacing:** the middle of the era (167–172) had few decisions. With 2 Attention pledged to office and school duties, most turns were one craft plus one attend.
- **Grain fleet:** the lapse message only showed in the log. It was easy to miss while waiting.

## Rating
**8/10.** The arrival prose paid off my specific choices (the promise, the forge, the Hospice) better than I expected. The `why` system makes the era legible. I'm docking points for the jump bug that ate my first arrival, the hidden hospice cost, and a grindy second half.
