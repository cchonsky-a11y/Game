THE BUTTERFLY EFFECT — P0 Butterfly Test
========================================
Rome, AD 155. Your time machine failed and left you here. You have a little gold, what you know,
and a small circle of people willing to listen. Something is coming from the East in a few years.
When you're ready, the machine can carry you 250 years forward. What you leave behind will go on without you.

This prototype covers one era, one jump, and your arrival. When you leave Rome, you'll see what became of it, and the test ends there.

First, a choice. You can afford only one:
  choose fountain  — The Subura's fountain runs brown. Clear the lead pipe and reline the basin.
  choose workshop  — Equip the smith by the Porta Trigemina with a second forge and paid apprentices.
Type 'help' for commands.

The machine shudders. Decades pass in the dark...

— Recognition —
You climb out of the machine in AD 415. The Tiber smells the same. Near the Porta Trigemina you hear
hammers: the smith's workshop you funded has become a whole street of forges, and over the oldest
door hangs a painted sign with two fires. Down the street, the fountain you didn't choose was filled
in long ago. An old woman calls the spot 'fever corner'.

— Wrongness —
Something is wrong, and it takes you a while to name it: beyond the few things you recognize, this is
Rome almost exactly as the history books describe AD 415.

— Personal echo —
In an old house in the Subura you find a line carved over a door, in Demetria's Greek: 'For the one
who stayed.' They don't know it means you.

— Discovery —
The Physicians' Circle is gone. One inscription remains, built into the wall of a newer house.
